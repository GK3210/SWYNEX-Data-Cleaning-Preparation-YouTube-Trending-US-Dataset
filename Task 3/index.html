Key findings from the analysis (all data)

Full details in the Task 2 report.

  • Skewed views: mean 2.36M vs median 681K; 11% of rows are outliers.
  • Category: Entertainment has the most volume (24.3%); Gaming, Music and Film earn about 2x its median views.
  • Engagement: by median per-video like ratio, News & Politics (0.85%) and Sports (1.1%) are lowest. The overall ratio in the KPI and bar chart (total likes / total views) ranks categories differently.
  • Timing: 32.7% of videos were published 14:00 to 17:00 UTC; weekends have higher median views (752K vs 662K).
  • March 2018 shift: median days to trend rose from 3.3 to 10.9 and new videos per day fell from about 44 to 12.
Definitions. Video-day = one video on one trending day. Overall like ratio = total likes / total views for rows where ratings are enabled. Trending within 3 days = share of video-days published up to 3 days before trending. Averages are means across video-days and are skewed by viral outliers. Views are cumulative snapshots. Only videos that reached the trending list are included.
