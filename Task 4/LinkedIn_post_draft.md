Completed Task 2 of my Data Analytics internship with @SWYNEX Technologies! 📊

I performed Exploratory Data Analysis on a cleaned YouTube Trending Videos (US) dataset: 40,899 rows, 6,351 videos, using Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn).

🔍 Key insights:
1️⃣ Views are highly skewed: the average (2.4M) is 3.5x the median (681K), so medians tell the real story
2️⃣ Entertainment has the most volume (24%), but Music, Gaming & Film earn about 2x the median views
3️⃣ News & Sports get the lowest like ratios and the shortest time on the trending list
4️⃣ Weekend uploads are only 18% of trending videos, yet get higher median views
5️⃣ The trending list changed around March 2018: videos took 3.3 → 10.9 days to trend, and new entries fell from ~44 to ~12 per day
6️⃣ Views roughly double while a video stays on the list

⚠️ Anomalies found: 237 old videos revived (one trended 11+ years after upload!) and 67 controversial videos with more dislikes than likes.

💡 Key learning: always check the distribution before trusting averages, and question what a dataset leaves out.
